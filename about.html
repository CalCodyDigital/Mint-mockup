Meet Anne.

Anne Murray is the owner and manager of Mint. She trained at the Vidal Sassoon Academy in London and continues her development through advanced cutting and colour courses.

The salon offers hair cutting and colouring in a relaxed environment, with personal advice, complimentary refreshments and WiFi.

With a passion for hairdressing, Anne keeps up to date with techniques and trends to help each client find a look that suits them.
